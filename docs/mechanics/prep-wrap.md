# Prep & Wrap Modes 

## Overview 

At the start and end of every in-game day, the player enters one of two special game modes that bookend the day's main events. 

Both take place in the Player Character's Bedroom and share the same interactable scene, but serve opposite purposes.

=== "Prep Phase"

    !!! tip "Learn more about the Prep Phase Mode in the [Game Mode System][game-mode-system] page"

    ## Prep Phase 

    Occurs at the beginning of each day before the player heads out. 
    <br>
    Choices made in this game mode carry into the day's events.
    <br>
    The player can freely click interactable elements in the Bedroom. 

    Required interactions must be completed before the story can continue into the main narrative.
    <br>
    Optional interactions are available for players to explore but not mandatory. 

    ### Notable Interactions 

    The following are notable interactions the player can have in the Prep Phase. 
    <br> 
    These interactions may have minor or major changes throughout the days. 

    - **Wardrobe** — choose a top and bottom outfit. Required before leaving.
    - **Laptop** — pick up the laptop to carry it. Required before leaving.
    - **Wallet** — choose whether to bring it or not. Optional.
    - **Bed** — option to take a nap if the player woke up early, which adjusts the arrival time. Optional.
    - **Key** — the exit gate. Only becomes available once all required interactions are done.

=== "Wrap Phase"

    ## Wrap Phase 

    Occurs at the end of each day after returning home. Similar interactable bedroom scene. 

    Required interactions must be completed before the player can progress to the next in-game day. 

    Interactions in Wrap Phase can be more directly narrative heavy than those in the Prep Phase. 
    <br>
    Though in general less interactable elements will be available.
